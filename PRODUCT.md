# Fashion Lookbook

## Product truth

Fashion Lookbook is a visual styling study for browsing self-authored looks by
silhouette, texture, and mood. It is an editorial portfolio surface, not a
storefront and not a catalogue of third-party brands.

## First release

- Browse a seasonal set of original look studies.
- Filter by silhouette and atmosphere.
- Open a look card for its material notes and styling rationale.
- Make the visual artifact lead; copy stays concise.

## Evidence boundary

The first release uses self-authored sample look studies. It does not claim
commercial availability, brand affiliation, or fashion-industry trend data.

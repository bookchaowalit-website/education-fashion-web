# Design direction — Editorial Contact Sheet

Fashion Lookbook is a contact sheet from a small editorial studio: oversized
serif typography, ivory stock, near-black ink, vermillion registration marks,
and deliberate asymmetry. The look image is represented by authored tonal
panels and material notes so the interface remains self-contained.

- Palette: ivory `#f5f1e9`, ink `#171512`, vermillion `#b44a32`, mauve `#c7b7b0`.
- Typography: expressive serif display with restrained sans metadata.
- Structure: large lead look, offset look rail, then an editorial detail sheet.
- Interaction: filter chips recompose the contact sheet; selected look gets a
  full-bleed tonal stage and material annotation.
- Responsive rule: the offset rail becomes a horizontal filmstrip on mobile.
- Motion: filmstrip slides only on filter changes; no repeated card entrance.
